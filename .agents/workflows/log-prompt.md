# Log Prompt

Add a draft entry to `docs/prompt-log.md`.

1. Ask me for: the stage, the tool and model, and the exact prompt I used (or take it from this conversation if I say so).
2. Summarise the AI answer in two lines.
3. Write the row as `DRAFT: verdict pending`. Suggest what looked wrong, missing or risky, but do not decide the verdict.
4. Ask me for the final verdict (accepted, fixed, rejected) and the reason, then update the row.
5. Remind me of the target: 5+ entries, at least 2 where the first answer was wrong and the prompt was improved.
